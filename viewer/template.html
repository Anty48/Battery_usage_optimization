How to read this

Every hour the agent picks a set point ut: positive charges the battery (energy drawn through the campus connection), negative discharges it into the campus. The battery responds with Et+1 = Et + η·ut when charging and Et + ut/η when discharging, with η = √0.9.

The grid supplies Pgrid = D − S + u. Importing costs πt and emits ct; the agent minimises the effective price λt = ct + κ·πt (κ = kg CO₂/€), which combines both objectives with the 70/30 weights of the score.

The agent only knows the next 3 h of π and c: it forecasts the rest of the day (and the demand and solar) with ridge models and analog days fitted on historical data, solves a linear program with a safety margin on discharge and applies only the first hour. Hours with solar above demand are free surplus that charges the battery.

Use Day to follow a full cycle and Week to see the weekday / weekend pattern. The chart cursor and the "Now" panel are synchronised; hover over a chart to inspect any hour.
